# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One operator monitors and steers coding agents from a phone, tablet, or desktop. The operator often
checks work in short sessions, reads while away from the host, and needs to understand progress or
answer a request without operating a terminal UI.

## Product Purpose

Collie is the mobile-first control surface for agents running in Herdr. It prioritizes the agent
conversation, tool activity, approvals, and generated artifacts. Terminal access remains available
as an explicit recovery surface rather than the primary reading experience.

Success means the operator can quickly answer four questions: what the agent is doing, what changed,
what needs attention, and what message will be sent next.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, and capable. Collie should feel like a focused native tool with a small amount of warmth
from its existing identity. It should not imitate terminal styling when structured information is
available.

## Anti-references

- Raw terminal output as the main conversation surface.
- Dense monospace text that mixes messages, commands, status, and tool results.
- Decorative dashboard cards or motion that competes with active work.
- Controls that depend on hover or hidden gestures on mobile.

## Design Principles

- Put the conversation first and keep terminal control one deliberate action away.
- Preserve the order and provenance of messages, tools, approvals, and images.
- Use progressive disclosure for noisy tool output while keeping failures visible.
- Keep the composer stable under mobile keyboards and preserve drafts across navigation.
- Degrade safely when a provider transcript lacks structured data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Support keyboard and touch input, visible focus, 44 px touch targets, browser
zoom, reduced motion, light and dark themes, and readable contrast in every semantic state.
