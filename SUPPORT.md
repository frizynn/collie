# Support

Choose the channel that matches the request:

- **Setup and usage:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/frizynn/nenu/discussions)
- **Reproducible Nenu defects:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/frizynn/nenu/issues)
- **Security vulnerabilities:** [private vulnerability report](https://github.com/frizynn/nenu/security/advisories/new)
- **Herdr behavior:** the [Herdr project](https://herdr.dev) and its own support channels

Before asking for help, check the [user guide](docs/USER_GUIDE.md),
[deployment guide](DEPLOYMENT.md), and [troubleshooting section](docs/USER_GUIDE.md#troubleshooting).

Share the Nenu version, Herdr version, OS, browser, installation method, deployment variant, and a
minimal reproduction. Sanitize logs and screenshots. Never post secrets, private pane output,
tailnet hostnames, tokens, or source code you cannot disclose.

Support is community-based and provided on a best-effort basis.
